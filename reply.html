Assalam-o-Alaikum meri jaan Alisha, Tumhara letter padhkar aankhein num ho gayi... itna pyaar, itni mohabbat, main kya kahoon? Tumne likha "Thank you" - lekin Alisha, shukriya toh mujhe kehna chahiye. Tum meri zindagi mein aayi, aur meri zindagi rang se bhar gayi. Tumhari har choti si khushi meri badi khushi hai. Tumhari har fight, har zid, har nakhra - sab mujhe tumse aur zyada pyaar karna sikhata hai. Jab tum kehti ho ke main tumhari life partner hoon, dil se dua hai ke yeh rishta hamesha aise hi rahe - respect, care, aur beshumar mohabbat ke saath. Woh watch jo tumne gift ki, usse har waqt apne paas rakhta hoon. Jab bhi waqt dekhta hoon, tumhari yaad aati hai. Woh sirf waqt nahi dikhati, humari mohabbat dikhati hai. I promise, main hamesha tumhari care karunga, tumhari respect karunga, aur tumhe kabhi bhi uncomfortable feel nahi hone dunga. Tum meri life partner ho, meri best friend ho, meri sab kuch ho. Thank you for being you. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for making my life beautiful.
