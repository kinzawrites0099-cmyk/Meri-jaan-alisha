🌹 Poetry for My Love 🌹

Alisha ke liye dil se likhi hui shayari

💝 Pehli Nazar
Pehli nazar mein dil tujhe de baithe,
Teri aankhon mein kho gaye hum,
Alisha tu meri zindagi ban gayi,
Tere bina ab kuch bhi nahi hum.

Tere hasne se din khil jata hai,
Tere rone se dil ro padta hai,
Tu meri jaan hai, tu meri pehchaan hai,
Tere sang har lamha suhana lagta hai.
💕 Mohabbat Ka Ehsaas
Mohabbat kya hoti hai yeh tujhse seekha hai,
Dil se dil ka connection tujhse hi dekha hai,
Alisha tu meri life ka wo hissa hai,
Jiske bina yeh dil adhura lagta hai.

Teri yaadon mein guzarta hai har pal,
Tere khayalon mein hota hai har lamha,
Door ho kar bhi paas lagti ho,
Tu meri duaon ka asar lagti ho.
💖 Tere Sang
Tere sang chalna hai zindagi bhar,
Tere sang hasna hai, tere sang rona hai,
Alisha tu meri manzil hai, mera rasta hai,
Tere sang hi toh yeh dil khona hai.

Chaand taare bhi sharma jayein,
Teri khoobsurti ke aage,
Tu meri zindagi ki sabse haseen,
Sabse pyaari, sabse khaas hai.
💗 Birthday Dua
Aaj teri birthday hai meri jaan,
Dua hai yeh din ho sabse khaas,
Har khushi mile tujhe is saal,
Har sapna ho tera poori aas.

Cake khilaunga, gift dunga,
Par sabse bada gift mera pyaar hai,
Alisha tu meri zindagi hai,
Tere sang hi mera aitbaar hai.
💘 Forever Promise
Waqt badle, haalat badle,
Par mera pyaar nahi badlega,
Alisha tu meri hamesha rahegi,
Yeh promise mera, yeh kasam hai.

Respect dunga, care karunga,
Har mushkil mein saath nibhaunga,
Tum meri life partner ho,
Yeh rishta hamesha bana rakhunga.

🎂 Happy Birthday Meri Jaan! 🎂

Alisha, tum sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho,
Tum meri best friend ho, meri life partner ho,
Meri har khushi ka reason ho.

Is birthday pe bas yahi dua hai ke
Tum hamesha aise hi muskurate raho,
Aur humara pyaar hamesha aise hi badhta rahe.

I Love You Alisha! ❤️
